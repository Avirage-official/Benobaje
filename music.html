Who's this guy

Hey, I'm AfroJunior 👋🏾

Born in Nigeria, grew up in Singapore. So yes, I can handle spice and I also queue for chicken rice like a pro.

Music started as a hobby when I was a kid. Hip-hop, pop, R&B, whatever I was feeling that week. Then I joined the Yeti Pack crew, we got pretty good, and Universal Music came knocking with an offer.

We said no.
Then we broke up.

Classic boy band arc, except we didn't even get the reunion tour.

I kept going though. These days I'm deep in Afro House and Afrobeats. Every track I make has a story in it, something you can actually connect with.

But I also need you to dance. That's the deal. You feel it, then you move. No standing at the back holding your drink.
