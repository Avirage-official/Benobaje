Nothing · Singapore · 2022

Phone (1) Launch

Bringing a hype-native tech brand into the physical world without losing the thing that made it hyped.

Role

Event Production · Brand Experience

Scope

Launch delivery · Customer journey · On-ground experience

Year

2022

Coverage

Techie Lobang ↗

01

The Insight

Nothing built its equity online — scarcity drops, design cult, community hype. The physical launch is exactly where brands like that usually get flattened.

A queue that drags, a handover that feels like a phone shop transaction, a space that looks like every other retail activation — any of it would contradict the identity the brand had spent a year building. In a launch, operations are the creative.

02

The Idea

Design the customer journey the way Nothing designs hardware: nothing unnecessary, nothing off-brand.

Every step between the street and the unboxing was treated as a brand touchpoint — arrival, queue, first look, handover — so that the person who queued online for months walked away feeling the same brand they’d been following.

“At a launch, the queue is the campaign. The handover is the ad.”
03

The Execution

  • Helped deliver one of the brand’s first major Singapore launches end to end
  • Built a seamless customer journey across every on-ground touchpoint
  • Protected Nothing’s premium, design-led identity through the operational details — flow, pacing, presentation
04

The Result

The hype survived contact with reality.

The launch landed as one of Nothing’s first major moments in Singapore — covered by local tech press, delivered without the brand’s identity cracking at a single touchpoint. Read the coverage ↗
