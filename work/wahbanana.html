Wahbanana · Singapore · 2023 — 24

Wahbanana

Short-form comedy engineered for the scroll — written, performed and tuned to the grammar of each platform it lived on.

Role

Comedy Content Creator — Platform-Native Content Strategy

Scope

Concept · Script · On-screen performance

Years

2023 — 2024

Watch

On YouTube ↗

01

The Insight

Comedy on social isn’t television cut shorter. Each platform has its own grammar — and a joke that ignores it dies in the first two seconds.

YouTube rewards setups that earn their runtime. TikTok punishes anything that doesn’t hook before the thumb moves. Instagram sits somewhere between. Shareability isn’t luck; it’s the product of writing for the mechanics of the feed — and for the very specific rhythms of Singaporean humour, which travels furthest when it’s most local.

02

The Idea

Build formats, not one-off videos — comedy engineered per platform, from concept to punchline.

Every sketch was designed backwards from how it would be found: the hook for the scroll, the beat structure for the watch-through, the tag for the share. Relatability was the distribution strategy.

“The algorithm doesn’t laugh. The audience does — the craft is writing for both.”
03

The Execution

  • Developed short-form comedy across YouTube, Instagram and TikTok
  • Carried each piece from concept and script through to on-screen performance
  • Engineered formats for reach, shareability and audience engagement
04

The Result

Comedy that behaved like content strategy — because it was.

Two years of platform-native writing and performing for one of Singapore’s best-known comedy channels: proof that engineering for the feed and being genuinely funny aren’t different jobs. Watch on YouTube ↗
